Leapsify Voice Agent · speech path · runs locally on the Mac Studio

From your voice to a spoken answer

You hold to talk on the phone or through the glasses. The audio goes to the Mac Studio, Whisper turns it into text, the agent picks tools and writes an answer with placeholders, code turns every number into natural spoken words, and Kokoro speaks it back sentence by sentence. No cloud model is in the loop.

What shapes the prosody today

  • Kokoro's af_heart voice brings its own learned rhythm and intonation.
  • Sentence splits set the pauses; very short fragments merge into the next sentence so speech doesn't stutter.
  • Answers stay at two short sentences, so the stress falls on the one fact asked for.
  • Numbers in words, with "about", sound like a person reading a screen, not a table.
  • Speed 1.05 keeps it brisk without rushing figures.

Why it feels fast

  • The filler line plays while the model is still deciding.
  • Speech is made one sentence at a time, so you hear the first one before the second is synthesised.
  • Whisper, the agent and Kokoro stay loaded and warm; the model's prompt is cached.

Not done yet

  • No emphasis or pitch control per word; Kokoro takes plain text.
  • Clarifying questions don't get a rising question tone on purpose; they rely on the "?".
  • No barge-in: you can't interrupt the answer by speaking.
  • The glasses haven't been tested end to end yet.

Conversation memory

Each turn leaves a small record written by code, not by the model: the calls that ran, the company, the option side, expiry and strikes, and what was said. Follow-ups are answered against that record.

  • "And the puts?" "What about next week?" The model picks what changed. Code carries the rest (same company, same expiry) from the last chain.
  • "Buy two of those." "The second strike." Code fills in the strike the last answer named, or that row of the chain. With no quantity it asks how many and drafts nothing.
  • Fresh numbers are reused. Asking again within a minute (longer for news and fundamentals) answers from the result you just heard. "Say that again" replays the last answer without the model.
  • It survives a reconnect. The last few turns and anything you asked it to remember ("call me Minh") are kept on the Mac for a day. "Start over" or "forget that" clears them.
